Updated October 25, 2026 At a glance curl --max-time 300 https://fundfactsapi.com/api/v1/funds/IE00B4L5Y983 \ -H "Authorization: Bearer ffk_your_api_key" The response is one JSON document: identity and key facts, TER, the SRRI risk indicator, a rule-based profile (category, risk band, concentration, region and sector tilt), top holdings, sector / country / asset-allocation breakdowns, calendar and annualised returns, a monthly performance series, volatility, Sharpe ratio and max drawdown — read from the documents the fund house publishes and refreshed every 24 hours. Endpoint What it does Requests GET /funds/{isin} Structured factsheet for one ISIN. 1 GET /funds/{isin}/history TER, KID costs, fund size, risk and top holdings over time, one point per as-of date (Pro+). 1 POST /funds Batch lookup, up to the plan's batch size per call. 1 per fund found GET /search Name, issuer, ISIN-prefix, ticker or WKN search: loaded funds, plus listed ETFs not loaded yet (coverage: unknown). free GET /resolve ISIN of an exchange ticker: CSPX on London, VWCE on Xetra (LSE, SIX, Xetra, Euronext). free GET /coverage Whether up to 100 ISINs are covered, before spending a request. free POST /portfolio Weighted look-through across funds (Starter+). 1 per position GET /overlap Holdings overlap between two or more funds (Starter+). 1 per ISIN GET /watchlist The funds you follow; POST adds, DELETE removes (10 funds on Starter, 100 on Pro). free GET /changes Change feed: tracked fields that moved between refreshes (your watched funds on Starter and Pro, every fund on Scale). free GET /webhooks Signed POSTs when a fund changes; manage endpoints (watched funds on Starter and Pro, every fund on Scale). free GET /export Every matching fund as NDJSON, optionally gzipped (Scale+). 1 per call POST /extract Turn any KID / KIID / factsheet PDF into the same JSON shape (Pro+). 5 per document POST /factsheets A one-page factsheet as HTML or PDF, saved with keyless share and embed links. 1 (HTML) · 2 (PDF) POST /reports A client's weighted fund list as one branded report: allocation, costs & charges, look-through, overlap, fund pages, sources.
