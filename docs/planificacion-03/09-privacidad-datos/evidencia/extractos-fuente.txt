[3] EDPB — Process personal data lawfully
If a data controller uses consent as a legal basis for the processing of personal data, they must ensure that this consent is freely given, informed, specific and unambiguous.

For consent to be unambiguous, there should be a clear affirmative action (without pre-ticked boxes and made separately from applicable general conditions).
